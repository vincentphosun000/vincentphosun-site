Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-07-30

Last updated / 更新日期：2026-10-11

1. Scope / 范围

This policy applies to vincentphosun.com and Vincent's private content tools, including Vincent Content Publisher and Vincent Data Insights, when they connect to TikTok or Instagram with the account holder's authorization.

本政策适用于 vincentphosun.com 及 Vincent 私人使用的内容工具，包括 Vincent Content Publisher 和 Vincent Data Insights；这些工具仅在账号持有人授权后连接 TikTok 或 Instagram。

2. Information processed / 处理的信息

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted, the tools may process account profile information, the account's published content and performance statistics, comments on its Instagram content, incoming messages sent to its Instagram professional account, and videos the user selects for TikTok draft upload. Comment and message data may include sender identifiers, text, and timestamps. The availability and history of platform data depend on the platform API.

依照接入的平台及实际授权范围，本工具可能处理账号资料、该账号已发布的内容及表现数据、其 Instagram 内容下的评论、发送到其 Instagram 专业账号的私信，以及用户主动选择上传为 TikTok 草稿的视频。评论和私信数据可能包含发送者标识、正文和时间；可取得的数据及历史范围以平台接口实际提供为准。

3. How information is used / 信息用途

The information is used to identify the authorized account, review content performance and audience feedback internally, and perform user-initiated TikTok draft uploads. The tools do not request platform passwords, automatically publish content, reply to comments, or send messages.

这些信息仅用于识别已授权账号、内部复盘内容表现与受众反馈，以及执行用户主动发起的 TikTok 草稿上传。本工具不索取平台密码，不自动公开发布内容、回复评论或发送私信。

4. Sharing / 分享

Personal information is not sold. It is shared only with the connected platform or technical service providers where necessary to deliver the authorized functions, or where required by law.

我们不出售个人信息。仅在实现已授权功能所必需时与接入平台或技术服务提供方共享，或在法律要求时提供。

5. Retention and deletion / 保留与删除

Comments on the account's published content may be retained for long-term content analysis. Incoming Instagram message text and sender and message identifiers are scheduled for deletion 89 days after first retrieval. Deletion is also checked before each new collection. If the local system cannot run at the scheduled time, expired records are deleted on its next successful run; physical deletion may therefore occur after 90 days while the system is unavailable. Only aggregate results without message or sender identifiers may be retained longer. Access may be revoked in the connected platform's settings. Access or deletion requests may be sent to the contact address already displayed on this site.

账号已发布内容下的评论可用于长期内容复盘。收到的 Instagram 私信正文、发送者和消息标识，计划在首次读取后第 89 天删除；每次重新采集前也会检查并清理。若本地系统届时无法运行，过期记录将在下次成功运行时删除，因此停机期间的实际物理删除时间可能超过 90 天。之后仅可长期保留不含消息或发送者标识的汇总结果。账号持有人可在相应平台设置中撤销授权，也可通过本站现有联系邮箱提出访问或删除请求。
