Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-07-30

1. Scope / 范围

This policy applies to vincentphosun.com and Vincent Content Publisher, a private TikTok workflow tool used by Vincent.

本政策适用于 vincentphosun.com 和供 Vincent 本人使用的私有 TikTok 工作流工具 Vincent Content Publisher。

2. Information processed / 处理的信息

After TikTok authorization, the tool may process basic profile information, public video lists, and videos selected for draft upload.

经 TikTok 授权后，本工具可能处理基础账号资料、公开视频列表，以及用户选择上传为草稿的视频文件。

3. How information is used / 信息用途

Information is used only to identify the authorized account, review its public content, and upload approved videos as TikTok drafts. The tool does not request TikTok passwords or publish automatically.

信息仅用于识别已授权账号、查看公开内容和上传 TikTok 草稿。本工具不会索取 TikTok 密码，也不会自动公开发布。

4. Sharing / 分享

Personal information is not sold or shared except as required to provide the authorized TikTok integration or comply with law.

我们不会出售个人信息。除完成已授权的 TikTok 功能或遵守法律所必需的情况外，不会向第三方分享相关信息。

5. Retention and deletion / 保留与删除

Authorization data is retained only as needed. Access can be revoked in TikTok settings. Deletion requests may be sent to contact@vincentphosun.com.

授权数据只在需要期间保留。用户可以在 TikTok 设置中撤销授权，也可以发送邮件至 contact@vincentphosun.com 提出删除请求。
